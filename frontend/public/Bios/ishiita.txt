Heyyyyy~! My name is Ishita Batra, but most people call me Ish.

Growing up, I was caught between two very different worlds. My father is a computer scientist who would casually rant about bugs, algorithms, and coding disasters at the dinner table. Meanwhile, my mother's side of the family somehow managed to explain half of economics to me using vegetables.

As a result, I spent my childhood hearing about software crashes and calculating profits on imaginary potatoes.

Like any curious kid, I started asking questions. Then more questions. Then enough questions to accidentally start an organization.

What began as curiosity slowly turned into Invisible Algorithm, a youth-led initiative dedicated to making technology and AI more accessible to students worldwide. Today, we organize workshops, conduct research, and connect students from across the globe through a shared love of innovation, curiosity, and occasionally questioning why the Wi-Fi stops working at the worst possible moment.

Turns out all those bug rants and vegetable lessons were useful after all. (still questioning it, kinda.)

Years ago, my family taught me profit-loss with potatoes. Today, we're teaching AI and business with workshops. We hope both are equally easy to digest. If not, #usecogniflow
