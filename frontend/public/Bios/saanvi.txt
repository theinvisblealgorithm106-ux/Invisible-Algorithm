Hi, my name is Saanvi, and I am the Executive Director of Creative Design as well as a student at Harold M. Braithwaite in Canada, pursuing the IB Diploma.
Believe it or not, there was a time when art meant absolutely nothing to me. Everything changed in Grade 5 when my school hosted an art contest, and I decided to give it a shot. As I created my very first piece, something clicked. For the first time, I felt like I had found a way to express myself without words!
That one moment sparked something incredible in me. Since then, I have taken numerous art courses, explored different styles and techniques, and earned several awards along the way. Every class, project, and competition has helped me grow not only as an artist but also as a person. Over time, I realized that art is so much more than drawing or painting it is about expressing who you are. Fashion, in particular, is one of the most powerful forms of art because it allows us to show our personality,and individuality without saying a single word. 
 That is why I am proud to serve as the Executive Director of Creative Design to encourage imagination, embrace innovation, and prove that art will always have a heartbeat as long as humans continue to dream, create, and share their stories with the world.
